# Product & Research + Project Management

Owns the "what" and the "when": scenario/category design, personas, competitor research, PRD, and cross-team coordination/timeline.

## Folders
- `scenario-design/` — category, subcategory, and scenario templates
- `personas/` — persona archetype definitions per subcategory
- `research/` — user research, market research
- `prd/` — PRD sections and drafts
- `competitor-analysis/` — Yoodli, Speeko, and other competitor breakdowns
